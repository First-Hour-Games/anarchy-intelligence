# Neighborhood clinic pass

## Gas station and road pass

- `GasStationLayout` rotates/repositions the existing furnished 6twelve shop, replaces its solid box collider with structural collisions, and hides showcase ground/walls and entry-door meshes.
- Forecourt has two pump islands, canopy, protection bollards, side-road entry and main-strip access, shop-front pavement and parking. Pump models are decorative custom geometry, not simulated dispensers.
- Flashlight placed on the shop counter for the planned story pickup; no inventory/quest logic added.
- Trees intersecting the station footprint are relocated nearby without changing their models. Other tree placements are unchanged.
- Shared asphalt material applied to street runs and both turnaround road meshes. Lower turnaround now uses the upper one's radius while accommodating its wider approach road.

- CyberThreat packs 1–3 are imported under `assets/local_licensed/cyberthreat_urban`; credit Conor McKenna (CyberThreat/Aphelios), CC BY 4.0. SOURCE.md includes source and license URLs.
