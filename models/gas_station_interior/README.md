# Gas station interior

The user supplied `GasStationInteriorAssetsv1.blend` and `Flashlight_Game.blend` for the gas station in `starting_forest.tscn`. Original files are archived unchanged under `source/`, which Godot ignores. No embedded Blender scripts are executed during export.

The GLBs are meter-scale modules with centered footprints and their bases at Y = 0. The flashlight points along Godot -Z, with its origin at the body center. Studio cameras and lights are excluded. `asset_dimensions.json` records exported dimensions and shelf elevations.

The supplied flashlight references four absent files in `Flashlight_Textures`: BaseColor, Metallic, Roughness, and Normal. Its missing baked material is replaced with a dark anodized metal finish. Body, clip, lens, and LED geometry remain from the supplied model. Cooler glass and the flashlight lens use alpha transparency in the exports.

The editable layout is `scenes/environment/gas_station_interior.tscn`, instanced at `GasStation/Interior` in the starting forest. The current stationV2 shell remains visible with its original wall, floor, and ceiling textures. Collision follows that imported room, foundation, porch, and side glass; an open entrance door and front glass complete the room. The reusable base station scene is unchanged. The builder places furnishings at the stationV2 floor elevation and preserves real-world prop scale despite the building instance scale.

The checkout flashlight uses `scenes/items/counter_flashlight.tscn`, with a real spotlight and the existing inventory pickup. It is lit until collected. Counter, inventory, held view, and pickup inspection all instance `scenes/items/flashlight_visual.tscn`. That shared visual supplies a readable dark metal finish without replacing the provided geometry. The held view has a light restricted to its model layer; the inventory and inspection previews have independent framing.

The left checkout is one continuous counter along the left wall, facing customers, with open cashier space behind it. The added rear room, tall storage and partitions are removed; customer coolers stay along the back wall. `gas_station_interior.gd` powers down station lights, neon ambience and emissive surfaces using local material copies, preserving the reusable station asset. The counter flashlight supplies the only station illumination; after pickup the player's flashlight lights the scene. Ceiling fixtures remain as unlit decoration.

`GasStation/Interior/Encounter` stages a pickup scare: raise the flashlight, reveal the previously invisible idle Ridgeback, cut to a close-up, play the real attack/lunge, and flare. The attack presentation lasts 1.28 seconds (0.50 seconds longer); the real clip plays more slowly to preserve its motion. The shot follows the animated head at the death scene's 62-degree framing and 0.85-metre initial distance, using the existing player camera so its fog follows correctly. A white flash masks the actor disappearing. There are no opening doors, notice or running-away clips. The attack cue preserves the original loudest 1.10-second RMS window of the supplied pig recording, with 0.50 seconds more source lead-in (62.53–64.13 seconds), normalized with short edge fades; see `sounds/entities/gas_station/README.md`. Normal AI and damage are disabled for this actor; other enemies in the shared monster group are suspended during the sequence. Health and the actual death/retry flow are untouched. It plays once per pickup; loading with the flashlight already owned suppresses the encounter.

For the current game preview, `trailer_enabled` is on by default. When the full scream finishes, a cut through black leads into a ten-second aerial flight over the station, forest road, silos and welcome center. It uses the existing `img/menu/title.png` logo, then fades to a black title card that holds as the preview ending. The separate camera has its own twilight environment and temporary moonlight; gameplay lighting is preserved. Gameplay HUD and scene audio remain suspended until Reset or Skip. The editable `TrailerOutro/Shots` camera and LookAt markers use forest coordinates; `flythrough_seconds` and `fade_seconds` tune the flight. Set `trailer_enabled` off to recover to playable first-person control after the scare (2.16 seconds total). Camera pose/current camera, FOV, near plane, HUD, held model, beam/spill intensity, audio pause states and prior input ownership are restored on completion, reset or skip.

In a debug build, press backtick/tilde to open the existing Debug Menu. Under **GAS STATION ENCOUNTER**, Preview moves the player to the checkout and plays the pickup scare and title flythrough, Reset restores the local pickup and removes the flashlight from inventory, and Skip grants it and restores control, including from the held title card. Reset does not reset broader story progress. Timing, close-up distance/FOV/lighting, flare intensity, and camera flinch are exported on the Encounter node; HiddenPosition, RevealPosition, AttackPosition, and PreviewPosition are editable markers. Its `phase_changed` and `finished` signals are available for later story integration.

Shelf products are stored in five MultiMeshes, including their transform buffers, to keep the 300-plus product placements inexpensive. Wall shelves open toward local -Z, while packaging labels face +Z: wall bays face into the room and their labels turn outward. Imported furniture stays instanced and can be moved directly in the Godot editor; the interior groups separate Architecture, Furniture, Merchandise, Details, and Lighting. Merchandise uses interior coordinates, so move its stock along with its shelf when changing the layout.

To rebuild the assets, run from the project directory (substitute installed executable paths):

```powershell
& '<Blender.exe>' --background --disable-autoexec --python tools/blender/export_gas_station_assets.py -- models/gas_station_interior/source/GasStationInteriorAssetsv1.blend models/gas_station_interior/source/Flashlight_Game.blend models/gas_station_interior
& '<Godot-console.exe>' --headless --editor --path . --import --quit
& '<Godot-console.exe>' --headless --path . --script res://tools/build_gas_station_interior.gd
& '<Godot-console.exe>' --headless --path . --script res://tests/gas_station_interior_smoke.gd
& '<Godot-console.exe>' --headless --path . --script res://tests/gas_station_encounter_smoke.gd
```

Rebuilding the layout replaces manual changes to the interior scene. Update the builder when those changes should be reproducible. Regressions check the actual starting forest, player capsule clearance and walking through the entrance, counter surface contact, saved product placements, power outage, actor visibility, pickup, sound assignment, nonlethal animation staging, completion/reset/skip, camera restoration, inventory headings, and the shared model. `player_flashlight_smoke.gd` now checks the established F-anytime behavior: selecting another inventory item keeps the light on, and pressing F switches it off.
